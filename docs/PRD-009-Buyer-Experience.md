# PropertyPilot

## PRD-009

# Buyer Experience

Version: 1.0

Status

APPROVED

Priority

MISSION CRITICAL

##############################################################

VISION

##############################################################

The buyer experience is the product.

The AI exists to enhance the showing.

It should never distract from the home.

The buyer should forget they are using software.

Instead, they should feel like they have a knowledgeable private guide available whenever they need one.

##############################################################

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

##############################################################

One Website

One Conversation

No Login

No Learning Curve
