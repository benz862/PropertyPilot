# Marketing Content Engine

# PURPOSE

The Marketing Content Engine automatically generates marketing

assets from the Digital Property Twin.

Every asset is derived from verified property knowledge.

The engine creates consistent, reusable marketing content across

multiple channels while maintaining brand standards and factual

accuracy.
