# ENG-019

# Property Health Engine

Version 1.0

---

# PURPOSE

The Property Health Engine continuously evaluates the completeness,

quality and usefulness of every Digital Property Twin.

Rather than measuring only listing quality, the engine measures

overall property intelligence.

Property Health is recalculated automatically whenever knowledge,

media, buyer behavior or marketing changes.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Objective.

Transparent.

Explainable.

Actionable.

Real-time.

Continuously improving.

Never arbitrary.

# OVERALL SCORE

Property Health

0–100

Composite score derived from weighted categories.

The score is intended to guide improvement rather than rank

properties against one another.
